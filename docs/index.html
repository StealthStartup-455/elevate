Product

Climbing gym software that brings both sides of the visit together.

Elevate is built for the rhythm of a climbing gym: staff manage arrivals and operations at the desk while climbers check their status, plan a visit and stay connected in the app. Both experiences work from the same gym information.

Features

Purpose-built for the front desk and every climber.

Unlike a desk-only workflow, Elevate connects the tools your team uses with the app climbers use—so check-ins, waivers, memberships and events stay in sync.

For your front desk

Your team's admin.
All in one place.

Give staff a real-time view of arrivals, membership and waiver status, and the next action to take—without leaving the desk.

  • Check-in, at a glance. Search, scan, or manually check in climbers with their pass and waiver status in view.
  • Climber records that travel with the day. Open a focused member panel, update details, review passes, and keep the queue moving.
  • A practical point of sale. Sell memberships, punch cards, day passes, rentals, and chalk; tender cash or card, void a sale, cash out the day, and review monthly-payment details.
  • Events your whole gym can see. Set hours, plan classes, events, and wall resets, then see sign-ups without a separate calendar.
  • Waivers with a clear record. Publish versions, view signatures, and open the signed document from the desk.

Climber app

For every climber

A better gym day
starts before they arrive.

Elevate gives your gym an app your members can understand in seconds: what is open, what they need to do, and what is happening next.

  • Ready-to-climb status. A member sees their membership, waiver, and check-in code in one clear place.
  • Hours and busyness. Show open hours, live attendance, typical busy times, and wall-setting notices before the trip to the gym.
  • Membership in their hands. Let climbers review their plan, passes, waiver, mock monthly-payment setup, and purchase history—and use the demo checkout for memberships, day passes, punch cards, rentals, and chalk.
  • Classes and events worth showing up for. Publish upcoming events, let climbers sign up for classes, and make the full calendar easy to explore.
  • One connected check-in. A climber can scan their code; the desk gets the arrival right away.

How much does Elevate cost?

Pricing for live gym operations has not been published. The hosted demo is free to explore; contact the team for updates.

What can I try in the live demo?

You can explore check-in, climber records, point of sale, gym calendar management, waivers, and the climber app’s home, check-in, membership, classes, and events views.

Is this ready to run my gym?

No. Elevate is a hosted early preview. The demo uses fictional, seeded data and is not for live operations or real climber information.

Does the demo process payments?

No. Its checkout experiences demonstrate the workflow only. They never collect card details or charge a payment.

How do I share feedback?

Email your feedback to elevate.climbing.team@gmail.com. Do not include passwords, personal information, or real customer records.
