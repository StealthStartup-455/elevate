- **Decisions and why:**
  - **Demo mode only** (review of #126): the mock checkout hands out a pass without taking money, so the Purchase tab, the page and `buyOnline` are on only when `isDemoMode()` is true. On a real deployment `buyOnline` refuses, the tab is hidden, and the dashboard's Purchase Membership and Renew membership buttons are switched off ("Coming soon"). Remove the gate when real payments land (#31, #45).
  - **The climber's own gym, not `getGym()`** (review of #126, after #34 and #123): `buyOnline` and the Purchase queries take the gym from the signed-in climber's record (`getMyGym`, then the cached `getGymById`), and `saveSale(input, { gym, staffId })` takes the gym (and the staff id) from its caller: `recordSale` passes the signed-in staff member's gym and id, `buyOnline` the climber's gym and no staff id. A climber can't buy another gym's products.
  - **Tax follows `dev`:** GST on the subtotal, PST on lines that charge it (rentals and chalk), recorded as `gstRateBps/gstCents/pstRateBps/pstCents` and `staffId` on the sale (`saveSale` was ported to this after the merge). The checkout shows GST and a PST row when there is PST. Memberships and passes are GST only.
  - **Rentals and chalk are sold to everyone, members included; passes and memberships are not sold to an active member** (`canBuyOnline(status, kind)` in `catalog.ts`). The Membership section is hidden for an active member, and `buyOnline` refuses a hand-made request for a pass too. This replaces the earlier "renew early" option in the app; the desk can still sell a membership to an active member. If active members should be able to renew near the end of their term, change `canBuyOnline`.
  - **One product per purchase, with a quantity for rentals and chalk; no cart.** A rental and a pass can't be bought together. The pure cart rules and `priceCart` are reused; `saveSale` (`checkCart`) holds the quantity line: one for a pass, 1 to 20 otherwise.
  - **The climber id comes from the session, never the request.** The action takes only a product id, a quantity and a key. Changing the quantity makes a new key.
  - **`ONLINE` is a separate tender,** not `CARD`, so the desk can reconcile the card machine and till without online mock sales mixed in.
  - **It is a mock:** no card details are asked for, nothing is charged, and the popup says so. Real payments are #31 and #45.
  - **`saveSale` refuses a replayed key that belongs to another climber** (found while sharing the code; the desk's replay never checked who the sale was for).
  - Hidden for gyms whose `system` isn't `ELEVATE`, like the desk POS tab.
