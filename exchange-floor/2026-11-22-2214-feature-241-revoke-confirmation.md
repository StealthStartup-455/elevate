- **Decisions and why:**
  - The reason is stored on `Pass`, not only logged: #236 asks for "a record if the member asks", and logs leave out staff-typed text (as `voidSale` does).
  - Revoke skips passes already revoked. A revoked early renewal ends where it starts (in the future), so without this a second revoke replaced the first reason.
  - The reason is on `DeskClimberPanel` and `PosClimber` (one loaded climber, like `bankAccount`) only, not on `Climber`: `getClimber` also feeds the climber's own `/me` pages, and the reason is for staff.
  - Who may revoke is unchanged (any desk staff). #236's "managers only" is separate work; this doesn't touch roles, so it won't collide with it.
- **Gotchas:** "Back to POS" after a sale clears the picked climber, so the e2e picks them again before revoking. The shared demo desk login has no staff id, so `revokedById` is empty for it.
- **Open follow-ups:**
  - **Revoke is in the wrong place.** "Revoke membership" sits on the POS climber card, but it's an account action, not a sale; it belongs on the climber's page under Customers. It was left on the POS here on purpose, so it can be moved when it's made manager-only (#254, from #236 request 4). `StaffRole` already has `MANAGER` and `OWNER`, and `requireStaffRole` exists in `src/features/auth/staff.ts`; whoever restricts revoke should move the button too.
  - The scan and check-in messages still say "Membership ended <date>" for a revoked membership; only the panel says "Revoked". Restricting revoke to managers is #254.
