- **Decisions and why:**
  - **Badge is the pass that let them in at the time** (`badgeAt`): `CheckIn` doesn't record it, and passes change after the visit (a day pass ends at midnight, the visit may use the last punch). Waiver and Message are as of now, because that is what the desk acts on.
  - `badgeFor` copies `checkInClimber`'s order (membership, day pass, punch card); `checkInClimber` itself was left unchanged on purpose. Change both together.
  - Filter list filters only the rows on the page, in the browser (as asked). No sort arrows: they would look clickable and do nothing.
  - Undo check-in and a whole-day name filter were built, then removed as out of scope for now.
  - The climber panel is #169. `FrontDeskBusyness.tsx` is no longer used by any page but was not deleted (needs the developer's OK).
  - Measured on the local database: all `/desk` queries together take about 10 ms, so the time is in rendering, not the database. Production build: a nav click to Check-in fell from about 100 ms to about 77 ms. `npm run dev` takes about 900 ms per first click (no prefetch, compiles on demand); with `loading.tsx` and `NavLink` the nav reacts in 33 to 70 ms there too. The saved round trip matters most on staging, where each one goes to Supabase.
  - Blue, not teal or slate blue, for charts: teal is actions and slate blue is navigation, so data gets its own hue. Steps come from the dataviz skill's validated sequential blue and pass its validator (`--ordinal`) against our light (#FFFFFF) and dark (#1C2022) surfaces.
